What Nima does today

Budgeting that keeps up with real life.

Most people stop tracking their spending because typing every purchase takes too long. Nima removes the typing. Everything below is live in the App Store today.

Voice entry

Say what you spent, in your own words. Nima fills in the amount, category, date and description.

Receipt photos

Snap a receipt and get a ready-to-check transaction back, merchant and total included.

You always confirm

Nothing inferred is ever saved silently. Every draft goes through a review screen first.

Clear reports

See where your money goes by category and by spending group, and how it changes month to month.

Budgets & savings goals

Split your income between essentials, wants and savings, and follow each goal as it fills up.

Works offline

Local-first. Log anything without a connection; Nima syncs in the background when you're back.

How it works

Claude does the reading. You stay in control.

Nima uses Anthropic's Claude models to turn unstructured input (speech, photos, documents) into structured, reviewable transactions.

01 — CAPTURE

Speak, snap or type

Speech recognition runs on the device first, so the common case is fast and private.

02 — EXTRACT

Claude structures it

Claude reads the transcript or the image and returns the amount, merchant, category and date, and says how sure it is.

Claude Haiku 4.5

03 — CONFIRM

You approve it

Low-confidence fields are flagged for review. Only what you confirm is saved.

In testing · next release

Bank statement import

Upload a month's bank statement as a PDF and review every transaction on one screen. This is how Nima reaches people in markets where automatic bank sync barely exists.

Claude Sonnet
  • Reads full statements from Mexican banks and fintechs, credit and debit.
  • Checks itself: totals are reconciled against the statement's own printed figures, and mismatches are surfaced instead of silently guessed.
  • Flags duplicates, transfers and instalment purchases before anything is written.
  • Validated against real statements from five Mexican issuers.

Privacy by design: voice recordings, photos and statements are processed in memory and never stored by Nima. Only the transactions you confirm are kept.

Where we're heading

From a budgeting app to a personal finance assistant.

Logging transactions is the foundation. Once Nima knows your money well, it can do much more than show you charts. We're building for three groups whose finances generic budgeting apps serve poorly.

Corporate workers

Make every paycheck go further

"I earn well, so why is there never anything left at the end of the month?"

  • Know what's truly free to spend after fixed costs each pay period
  • Bring benefits, savings funds and bonuses into one picture
  • Set goals that adjust automatically as income changes
Freelancers

Steady footing on uneven income

"Some months are great, some are tight, and taxes always catch me off guard."

  • Smooth irregular income into a monthly budget that holds
  • Keep business and personal spending apart
  • Know how much to set aside for taxes before it's due
Entrepreneurs

Clarity across business and personal

"My business money and my own money are tangled together."

  • See cash flow for the business and the household side by side
  • Turn receipts and statements into reports for your accountant
  • Spot what's draining cash before it becomes a problem

The assistant layer

Ask about your money in plain language.

Built on the same Claude foundation, the assistant will answer questions about your own finances, write a short monthly summary of what changed, and warn you about problems before they happen.

Can I afford a 6,000 peso trip next month?
Yes, if you keep restaurants near last month's level. You'd still reach your emergency-fund goal by March, two weeks later than planned.

Why we started in Mexico

An underserved market, on purpose.

Most finance apps are designed around automatic bank sync, and in Mexico that barely works. People manage money across cash, debit, several credit cards and fintech accounts. Nima was built for that reality: fast manual capture first, statement import next.

01
Spanish first, not translated Mexican Spanish from day one, including how people actually talk about money.
02
No bank sync required Voice, photo and statement import work with any bank, card or wallet.
03
Built to travel Currency, timezone and language come from the device, so the rest of Latin America is a configuration change, not a rewrite.
